Speech & Audio Machine Learning

StutterSense · Annotator-Aware Stuttering Event Detection

Oct. 2026 · PyTorch · WavLM-base-plus · SEP-28k · Calibration & Uncertainty

Disfluent Speech
  • Developed a multi-label detector for five stuttering-event types using frozen WavLM representations. Preserved three annotators’ votes as majority labels, soft vote proportions, and ambiguity targets.
  • Scanned 21,856 SEP-28k entries and sampled 2,000 clips (1,400 train / 300 validation / 300 test). Compared hard-label BCE, soft-label BCE, BCE+Brier regularization, and ambiguity multi-task learning across three seeds.
  • BCE+Brier achieved mean macro-F1 of 0.432 ± 0.025; soft-label BCE attained macro average precision of 0.422 ± 0.001. Paired-bootstrap intervals did not establish a reliable F1 advantage over hard-label BCE.
  • Examined per-event errors, calibration, soft-target Brier scores (0.0609 ± 0.0005 for soft BCE versus 0.0641 ± 0.0010 for hard BCE), and risk–coverage. Speaker-independent separation could not be verified from the hosted metadata.

MicCheck · Recording-Condition Leakage in Speech Representations

Oct. 2026 · AMI Corpus · Cepstral/Spectral Features · SVM · Domain Adaptation

Speech Robustness
  • Created 2,000 matched close-talk (IHM) and distant-microphone (SDM) examples from 10,595 discovered AMI pairs spanning 21 speakers and 18 meetings to measure recording-condition information.
  • Condition probes achieved 95.52% speaker-held-out and 94.92% meeting-held-out mean five-fold balanced accuracy. Accuracy remained 95.16% after removing energy and duration cues.
  • Compared feature ablations, normalization, PCA whitening, and separately reported target-aware CORAL; assessed closed-set speaker transfer and limited lexical transfer across recording conditions.
  • Validation-selected paired alignment (λ = 0.1) reduced pairwise embedding distance by 27.89%, yet condition-probe accuracy decreased only 0.87 percentage points, showing that alignment alone did not remove recoverable condition information.
