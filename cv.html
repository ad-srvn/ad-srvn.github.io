Neural Localization in Non-Linear Stochastic Environments

SNNs · Particle Filtering · STDP · LIF Neurons

State Estimation
  • Designed a recurrent Spiking Neural Network particle filter for Bayesian localization, representing candidate states as particles encoded through neuronal voltage and spike timing.
  • Developed phase encoding for state, motion, and noisy observations and replaced explicit resampling with Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity to update posterior weights from temporal correlations.
